Our Services

Select a service to explore the process.

Event ManagementFrom the first brief to venue handover.

  1. Set the brief

    Define the audience, purpose, budget and what a successful event should achieve.

  2. Build the plan

    Agree on the venue, programme, cost breakdown and approval dates.

  3. Bring the team together

    Confirm suppliers, floor plans, crew responsibilities and the master schedule.

  4. Open the doors

    Brief the team, rehearse transitions and coordinate guests, vendors and programme changes on site.

  5. Close the project

    Hand the venue back, reconcile costs and review the outcome with the client.

What you receive

An event plan, itemised budget, master schedule and close-out report.

Concert ManagementFrom the artist brief to the final load-out.

  1. Map the show

    Confirm the line-up, venue, audience capacity and performance schedule with the promoter.

  2. Advance the production

    Review artist riders with the venue and suppliers. Agree on stage layout, backline, audio and lighting.

  3. Load in & soundcheck

    Schedule equipment arrivals, stage setup, line checks and soundchecks before doors open.

  4. Run the concert

    Coordinate artist calls, stage changes and show cues while the venue team manages audience entry and exit.

  5. Load out & review

    Coordinate equipment returns, complete venue handover and review production notes with the promoter.

What you receive

A production schedule, artist requirements list, venue operations plan and show running order.

Event PlannerFrom an early idea to a plan ready for the crew.

  1. Discover the idea

    Identify the occasion, guest profile, priorities and spending limit.

  2. Shape the concept

    Compare venue options and propose a theme, guest journey and programme format.

  3. Develop the blueprint

    Build the floor plan, supplier brief, budget and planning calendar.

  4. Hand over the plan

    Walk the delivery team through the run sheet, decision points and contingency options before event day.

  5. Review the outcome

    After the event, compare client feedback with the original goals and capture ideas for the next edition.

What you receive

A concept proposal, cost estimate, draft programme, layout and planning calendar.

Event CoordinatorFrom taking over the brief to the final sign-off.

  1. Take the handover

    Collect existing bookings, vendor contacts and the latest programme from the organiser.

  2. Confirm the details

    Check access times, deliveries, guest arrangements and outstanding decisions with each lead.

  3. Brief & rehearse

    Issue the final run sheet, assign cues and rehearse the critical transitions.

  4. Coordinate the day

    Call vendors and speakers into position, track timing and relay changes to everyone affected.

  5. Wrap the site

    Confirm guest departure, vendor collection and venue handover, then report outstanding items to the organiser.

What you receive

An event-day run sheet, operations contact list and crew assignments.
